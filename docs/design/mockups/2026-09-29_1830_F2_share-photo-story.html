N1 · Share your photo + the combo

Final proposal, with the CEO's decisions of 2026-09-29 applied: the whole uploaded photo, face included; a preview sheet with "Incluir mi foto" (default ON); sneaker mode in this block. Still open for the CEO: the logo (N4), the "+ CREMA" fix (N3) and the copy. The figure is a drawn placeholder: no real photo is embedded in this public file. Story artboards use the real r13 frame size (432 × 768 logical = 1080 × 1920 px), scaled down.

1 · The photo story: the whole photo (CEO decision)

Headline, then the photo and its combo as one card (the whole photo on top, the band glued under it, so it reads as "this outfit → this combo"), then the logo. Nothing sits on top of the photo, so the combo and the logo are always readable, whatever the photo looks like. The photo is never cropped for any aspect from 5:8 to 16:9: the card takes the photo's shape inside a 392 × 415 box. Only photos taller than 5:8 (full-screen 9:16 captures, screenshots) lose a sliver, cut evenly top and bottom.

5 · N3 · "+ CREMA" wrap fix (drag the width)

The on-screen hero band is screen width − 90 wide (20 + 20 screen margin, 24 + 24 card padding, 2 border). With flex 130 : 150 : 90, the neutral segment is only 24% of that. "+ CREMA" needs about 79 px (tag + 2 × 8 padding), so it wraps on anything under about 405 dp. On the photo story, the band is as wide as the photo, so it can be as narrow as 259 (outfit) or 236 (sneaker, 2-line headline). Fix: tags always stay on one line, and each segment gets at least its tag's width; the rest is shared by flex. Last resort: shrink the tag, never to less than 0.8×. The sneaker band also needs shorter ca/en/fr tags (doc §7.3).
