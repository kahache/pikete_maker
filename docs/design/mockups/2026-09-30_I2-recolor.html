I2 · "Vérmelo puesto": recolor ONE region of your own photo with the proposed colour

UX proposal (CEO GO with conditions, 2026-09-30). One entry point on the D36 result; a no-scroll recolor view with a two-segment ARRIBA / ABAJO selector; press-and-hold shows the original; the exit is the D37 story sheet, reused untouched. The other region, skin, hair and background stay as shot. Everything on-device, ~instant.

Demo fit: ecru top (neutral) + rust pants (the BASE). Hero combo: tu fit rust · súmale blue · + crema. The default recolored region is the one that is NOT the base (the top), because "súmale" is the colour to add, and the base is what you keep.

1 · Entry point on the outfit result: one pill under the shareable card recommended

"Vérmelo puesto" sits directly under the D36 card, before "Otras combis". It is OUTSIDE the card (the card is the OFF-state story export: no chrome inside the shareable area) and above the fold on an 844 phone. It always applies the HERO combo (complementary), like the story does. The bottom CTA block stays at two pills.

tip line instead of the button · no dead end

No dead-end screen anywhere. Every "not applicable" reason is known at analysis time (person count, exposure, mask coverage), so the result simply shows a one-line tip in the button's slot: it teaches the feature and the fix, and it is never an error. Three variants of the same sentence: "…sal solo en la foto." (several people) · "…hace falta más luz." (dim scene) · "…que se vea tu fit entero." (region too small / mask unreliable). If only ONE region is unreliable, the button shows and the view opens on the good region with the selector hidden (we never announce what is missing, like S3).

2 · The recolor view: no scroll, selector and CTA in the thumb zone recommended

The card is the SAME object the story shares (photo scaled, never cropped, hero band flush under it). Switching ARRIBA / ABAJO is instant (both bitmaps are computed on entry, off the UI isolate; 200 ms crossfade). Press-and-hold anywhere on the photo shows the original while held: the standard "compare" gesture, one thumb, no slider. The headline is conditional on purpose: it is a preview, not a promise.

The chip on the photo is the only on-screen chrome over the photo and it never reaches the export. It reads "Mantén para ver el original" normally and "Original" while held. Release = back to the recolor with the 200 ms crossfade. No slider (two-handed, fiddly, and a 23 ms recolor makes instant swapping better than a wipe), no side-by-side thumbnails (halves the photo).

3 · The exit: "Súbela a tu story" opens the D37 sheet with the recolored photo recommended

Nothing new in the sheet: same preview, same "Incluir mi foto" switch (remembered), same privacy line, same "Compartir". The story frame is D37's, with two substitutions: the photo is the recolored bitmap and the headline is the view's own "Así te quedaría" (zero drift with the screen the user came from, and an honest marker for free). OFF shares the card-only image, where the recolor plays no part.
